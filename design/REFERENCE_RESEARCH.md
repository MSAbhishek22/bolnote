# BolNote — Reference Research (24 Nov 2026)

Five-plus concrete references researched before any frontend work
(Blueprint J8: ≥5 references across ≥3 sources).

1. **Granola** (via overtheanthill.substack.com/p/granola and
   lab.iriscocreative.com meeting-artifacts research) — calm notepad
   metaphor; AI-written text visually distinguished from the user's own
   (grey vs black); **every AI bullet links to the exact transcript
   moment** — one-click audit. Takeaway for BolNote: summary points and
   action items must link back to the transcript segment and seek the
   audio. Calm, no dashboard clutter.
2. **Descript** (descript.com guide; discoverai.tools workflow) —
   transcript-as-document: the text IS the interface; click a word to
   hear that moment; search replaces scrubbing. Takeaway: clicking any
   transcript segment seeks playback; text search highlights segments.
3. **Otter.ai** (otter.ai/pricing, read directly 24 Nov 2026) — the
   incumbent's structure: monthly minute pools, per-conversation caps,
   lifetime import caps, 6 transcription languages (no Hindi). Takeaway:
   BolNote's anti-positioning — no caps theatre, upload-first, and the
   pricing/limits honesty panel ("what this costs: your own free Groq
   quota") instead of a fake pricing grid.
4. **MeetMap** (CHI 2025, via the Iris Cocreative research piece) —
   academic evidence that a linear transcript misrepresents dialogue;
   spatial mapping of moments helps navigation. Takeaway: justifies the
   3D waveform-ridge timeline as a *navigation* device, not decoration.
5. **Google Recorder pattern** (recorder.google.com, product knowledge
   cross-checked against the category reviews read today) — search
   inside a recording, everything findable by word. Takeaway: BolNote's
   transcript search with hit-count and jump-to-hit is a core control,
   not an extra.
6. **Design galleries** (user's standing set: godly.website,
   land-book.com, dark.design) — dark, editorial, single-accent pages
   score highest for tools in this category. Takeaway: BolNote's
   "night radio studio" identity — ink-black ground, one warm amber
   accent (the ON-AIR lamp), no gradients, no glassmorphism.

## What we deliberately do NOT copy
- No meeting bot, no calendar integration, no team workspaces (Granola/
  Otter scope) — BolNote is upload-first and single-user.
- No transcript *editing* of audio (Descript's editor) — v1 is
  transcribe → read → search → notes, honestly scoped.
